## The four misses of the full run, and what each was traced to

1. `doc-fault-code`: Lantern's doing. The word "code" brought in MAKE CODE,
   and any MAKE tool that fitted put an open document into "make something
   from it". Fixed in 1.24.1. Passed three times since.
2. `code-ladder-start-stop` (failed on 1.24.1, not on 1.24.0): the same
   request, word for word, got two different first replies from the 35B. In
   the failing one it wrote rungs of its own with Stop in a branch. Traced to
   the example Lantern showed it: the Arduino start/stop sketch, for a ladder
   question. Fixed in 1.24.2; it now adapts the ladder example on the first
   try.
3. `make-music-waltz`: the send-back offered choices and came back unchanged.
   1.24.2 writes the bar to use; the 35B wrote it.
4. `make-music-ode`, `make-music-orchestra`: the tune from memory, with the
   4th bar's rhythm flattened, bar lines missing or at the ends of phrases.
   1.24.2 reads those bars as meant, and gives the model the tune's lines
   (the songbook). It copied them, 16 bars, on the first try.
