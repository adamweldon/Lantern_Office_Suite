## What changed (1.24.17)

  set in `C.turn`); older messages that do not fit kept as short versions,
  newest first. `C.turn` marks the person's questions and their words
  (`msgs.asked`, `msgs.askedText`, `msgs.askedFrom`): a question's short
  version is the person's words; an unmarked user message is Lantern's and is
  one line (`OWNMSG`, by how it begins, where there are no marks). An
  answer's blocks named in one line as `aiAct.find` takes them (it now gives
  each block's `from` and `to` lines), fences with them; 700 characters at
  most, never inside a block's line; an answer kept leaves room for its
  question; then the picture note.

  and long ton; a plain gallon, quart, pint or fl oz beside an imperial one is
  imperial (`IMPOF`), not beside its own imperial measure, and Lantern's own

  capitals of oz/lb/kg/g/km/cm/ft/yd/in/mi only; a few plain words (gals, qt,
  millilitres, millimeters, metric tonnes); the ounce exact; an unknown unit
