## What changed

- CORE: `Lantern.lists` (level, type, own, start, mark, css), the one rule
  for a list's style by its level; Word's letters past z as a counter style.
- STYLES: Writer's page shows lists by it.
- WRITER: Save .txt numbers items as the page does (and lines up their next
  lines, puts a list in a cell on the cell's line, white space as a browser
  shows it); Save .html carries the rules and each list in a list's type;
  paste keeps a list's style and start; Tab, Shift+Tab, Esc (see the
  CHANGELOG).
- PDF_DOC_LAYOUT / PDF_DOC: marks by level, start and value; second- and
  third-level bullets drawn; a list straight inside a list kept (it was left
  out of the PDF).
- DOCX_WRITE / DOCX_PACKAGE: each list's style at its level; Word's own
  levels for the rest. DOCX_READ: a level's own style left to the level.
- AI_DOC: the new example; a list goes on from the model's number; an item's
  sub-points as a stack of levels; the preview by the rule.
- GUIDE_WRITER_PRO: "Lists in lists".
