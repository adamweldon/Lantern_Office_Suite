## What is in it

The recipe with the question's numbers in it. Where Lantern can be sure
which number goes in which blank, it fills the recipe card itself and gives
the line to copy (AI_NO_FORMULA's `filled`, and a new module AI_RECIPE_FILL
for a planet, a chemical formula, a change of units, bags, a tank, two
places, a height and a radius, a price for one, so much for so many, eggs).
It fills nothing for a number written as a word, a card's own exception
(cloudy water), or a unit the recipe cannot be said in; a question that
asks for minutes where the recipe gives hours gets the line inside a
CONVERT. Of the bench's 35 sums with one recipe it fills 31, each equal to
the bench's answer; of 73 other questions it fills 44, none wrongly
(`tests/recipe_fill.js`).
