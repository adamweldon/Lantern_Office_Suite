## What changed

- AI_CONTROL: the alternating relay (alternator AR M1 M2, or a coil called
  the alternating relay); floats read by their job; the lead and lag call
  relays, the alternating relay and the pumps wired by Lantern; a rung's
  branches of several contacts; the alarm light on the alarm float; a pump
  station's own instructions and example. Both of the 35B's blocks draw.
- AI_PUMP_STATION (new): MAKE PIPING (the wet well, inlet, pumps, each
  pump's check valve then shutoff valve in a valve vault, the header to the
  force main, an inverted J vent) and MAKE LEVELS (the wet well in section,
  the floats at their levels, to scale or in order). Sources: Recommended
  Standards for Wastewater Facilities (Ten States Standards, 2014) 42.52,
  44.4, 42.64; Ontario's Design Guidelines for Sewage Works (2008) 7.2.5.
- AI_PARTS (new): a question asking for several things is made one at a
  time, in Chat and on the bench's path (`aiAct.solve`).
- The bench: make-liftstation, Adam's own question, graded part by part
  (`parts` and `partCheck`, read by AI_BENCH's grade).
