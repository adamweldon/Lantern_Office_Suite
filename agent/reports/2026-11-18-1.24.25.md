# 2026-11-18 — 1.24.25: Sheets is edited as Excel is; POWER is Excel's, watts are POWERW

Started from `lantern-1.24.24`. Work in progress was kept on
`wip-2026-11-18-sheets-editing` until the gates passed.

## Adam's word

- 2026-11-18, on QUESTIONS 16 (POWER): "we need to keep the excel formula as
  it works in excel and lantern and make a change for the power in watts that
  lantern can discern that will work for the ai correctly that way we stay
  more excel compatible when we export an xls. Proceed with the next stage for
  sheets editing and merge into the public release once verified".
- So: POWER is Excel's; Lantern's watts are POWERW, with a check that hands
  the line back to a model that wrote POWER for watts. Main is to be merged
  when 1.24.25 is verified (his go-ahead for this version).

## What changed

- PHYSICS_CELLS: POWERW(energy, time). SHEET_FN_CORE: Excel's POWER (#NUM! for
  0^0 and a negative to a fraction, #DIV/0! for 0 to a negative). The catalog
  and the formula help say which is which.
- AI_CHECKS `powerMeantWatts`: reads the question's asking sentences, its one
  energy and one time (J, kJ, MJ, mJ, Wh, kWh, MWh, mWh; ms to days; "an
  hour", "half a minute", "per hour"); speaks only when a power in W or kW is
  asked and nothing else, and the reply's one POWER is its whole sum (or that
  in other units) with that energy and time. The line is POWERW(J, s), /1000
  for kW.
- SHEET: AA1 read as past Z; a range clamped at Z; a date typed as text takes
  its cell's number format; TRUE/FALSE shown for a yes or no (comparisons,
  AND/OR/NOT/XOR, the IS functions, IF/IFERROR/IFS/CHOOSE/SWITCH whose every
  answer is one); a range's numbers leave out TRUE and FALSE (SUM, COUNT,
  AVERAGE); Ctrl+End; hooks `onTyped` and a fifth argument (the formula's
  kind) to show hooks.
- SHEET_EDIT (new): a range (drag, Shift, Select range on a phone, headings,
  Ctrl+A, Ctrl+arrows), the active cell kept apart from the range's corners;
  copy, cut (moved on paste, references following, #REF! for cells pasted
  over, ended by any change or Esc), paste (Excel's tab text; tiling; Lantern's
  mark on the clipboard); fill; undo and redo; Delete keeping looks; Enter and
  Tab through a range; whole rows (2:2) and reversed ranges moved; references
  untouched keep their text.
- SHEET_FORMAT (new): bold, italic, alignment, Excel number codes per cell
  (`doc.fmt[ref].nf`; `nfT` when it came from typing), a formula's carried
  look (not for counts, comparisons, logicals, date parts; a date code only to
  a formula that gives a date), typed looks kept on their cell, General taking
  a typed $ away, decimals per part of a code, freeze top row.
- SHEET_ROWS (new): rows and columns in and out (looks of empty cells move;
  new ones take the row above's), sort in Excel's order keeping rows whole
  (a part of a table sorts the whole table), 100 more rows.
- The Sheets help: "Editing like Excel".
