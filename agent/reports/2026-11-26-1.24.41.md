# 1.24.41 -- a PDF's maker's limits said, and the work done (2026-11-26)

## What Adam asked
QUESTIONS 1 (since 1.24.39): what Lantern should do when a PDF's maker asks that it not be
printed, copied or changed. Adam (2026-11-26): "say so and do what the person wants."

## What changed
- The PDF tile asks nothing. The first time a tool does what the maker asked not be done
  (a change, a comment, a fill, the lock taken off, printing), a line under the toolbar
  says so ("Its maker asked that this PDF not be changed; done as you asked, in a new
  copy."); it stays while that PDF is open. What the maker asked is kept with the PDF as
  opened, so a changed copy (which has no lock) still says it when printed.
- "Take the lock off" works on a lock with limits without the owner's password.
- The tile's help says the same; CLAUDE.md has Adam's answer; QUESTIONS 1 is gone.

  PDF its owner locked against everything go ahead with no question, the line says both,
