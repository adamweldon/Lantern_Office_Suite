## 1.24.4: the Health tile, audited

What was done is in the changelog. How it was done matters for the next
tiles:

- The tile was used in Chromium first (everything opened; the ORS mixer had
  two faults).
- The lessons were drafted from the standard guidance, then **four separate
  checkers**, none of which had seen the drafting, each took a share of the
  lessons and checked every figure and instruction against sources they
  opened themselves. They were told to find what was wrong, not to confirm.
  They found some thirty things to change in the new drafts, and **13 in
  the lessons the tile already had**, which nobody had asked about. Every
  finding was applied or is listed below.
- Then the figures were pinned in `tests/health.js`, so a later edit that
  changes one fails a test.

What a person can do now that they could not: find what to do for someone
not breathing, choking, bleeding, in shock, fitting, poisoned, drowned,
burned, knocked on the head; tell whether a child's breathing is fast for
its age with a counter that times the minute; tell whether a child is
dangerously thin with a strip of paper; care for diarrhoea beyond the
recipe; help at a birth when nobody trained can come; build a latrine and a
hand-washing tap; work out bleach for a drum of water; print any lesson.
What a small model can do now: answer "my son is choking" from the Choking
lesson, whole, instead of from memory.

## Found, and left

- **The public download (`main`, 1.14.3) still carries the old Health
  lessons**, nine of which the check found wrong or out of date. The one
  that matters most: "Keeping food without a fridge" tells people to dry
  meat and fish in the sun as they are and to remove "the air" (botulism
  grows without air). Only Adam publishes.
- The checkers could not open every source. Read through copies, not the
  original: WHO's 2005 diarrhoea manual, WHO's pregnancy and childbirth
  guide (chapters on NCBI and a national adaptation hosted by WHO), the IMCI
  chart booklet (a transcription). No source was opened for: wetting the
  lips in shock; keeping both eyes still for an eye injury; the entry and
  exit burns of an electric shock; the blanket-and-poles stretcher and the
  clothes drag (the Army manual's own pages were only partly opened); "do
  not straighten" a broken limb beyond St John's "do not force it back";
  whether to sponge a fever where there is no medicine at all (NHS and NICE
  say no; the lesson follows them).
- Where the sources disagree the lesson takes the more cautious and says so
  in its module header: 5 breaths first for a child (European) with "pushes
  alone still help" (American); boiling 3 minutes above about 1,500 m (the
  EPA's height, lower than the CDC's); "no bath on the first day" (WHO 2017
  over the older 6 hours).
- No pictures. No medicine (questions for Adam). Not read by a nurse, a
  midwife or a first-aid instructor.
- The Food tile's `food_preserve` lesson had the same faults and was
  corrected; the rest of that tile is unchecked and is next.
- `Lantern.suite.passages(..., {strong: true})` looks only at the best hit
  (queue item 7).
- The 2B's "400 Bad Request" on three puzzles: cause not yet known; the

- A cloud session can fetch the engine (`sh kit/setup-kit.sh engine` works
