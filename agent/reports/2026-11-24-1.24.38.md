# 2026-11-24 — 1.24.38: Lantern as a library

Branch `lantern-1.24.38` (from `lantern-1.24.37`). Work branch
`wip-2026-11-24-library`.

## What prompted it

Adam, after the 1.24.37 fixes, on how Lantern and the model share the work:

- he asked whether Lantern combines a neural model with symbolic tools;
- he asked for a "do your homework" reminder and a Dewey-decimal-like catalog,
  with tools offered when the subject comes up;
- he asked for a percentage of surety from keywords;
- he asked that the methods be tested to find "the least action path to the
  answer at the boundary of the two systems";
- then: "a reasoning engine doing the reasoning with calculators and tools
  that present the easy button ... If that makes sense to you proceed."

The plan went to him first as a PDF (two A5 pages).

## What changed

See CHANGELOG 1.24.38:

- **LIBRARY_CATALOG.** Ten classes and 33 shelves, built from what Lantern
  holds. The surety is measured: per kind of evidence and per shelf, counted
  on 984 labelled questions.
- **The library prompt** (Prompt size: Library):
  - every question starts with the same words;
  - the shelf is named only at 8 in 10 ("most likely") or 9 in 10 ("on");
  - lessons Chat gives are said as found, not claimed;
  - where Lantern is not sure, the model may choose a shelf with OPEN.
- **OPEN.** A Chat command, read narrowly. It hands only what Chat's own sure
  reading finds on the shelf.
- **keeps().** How long a cooked or kept food keeps gets the Food lesson, in
  every prompt (the rice row).
- **The bench.** Every push runs each small model with `auto` and with
  `library` (`.github/bench-prompts`).
