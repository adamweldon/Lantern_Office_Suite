# 2026-11-23 — 1.24.35: float switches the right way up

Branch `lantern-1.24.35` (from `lantern-1.24.34`). Work branch
`wip-2026-11-23-floats`.

## What prompted it

Adam, on 1.24.34's lift station ladder: the float switches were drawn as if
a falling float closed them ("they would open the circuit when the float level
got higher stopping the pumps from running"). And his answers to the reviews'
questions: LS1 is typically a limit switch, FS1 a float switch; with probes
Stop, Start, High, High starts the second pump and the alarm float is highest.

## What changed

See CHANGELOG 1.24.35: float, pressure and temperature switches drawn NO
under the line (closing on rise) and NC resting on the terminal from above;
labels and branch-row tags clear of the float; Draw's palette has them; a
plain LS with no name, or named for a hatch, door, valve, open or closed, is a
limit switch, and an LS named for a float's work is still a float.

## Review

One, 192 cases (`tests/fixtures/float_review_1235`). Its 21 high findings were
all in a name rule of the first draft (an e-stop, an On/Off switch, a Lead/Lag
selector read as floats; LS floats named "Pump 1 On", "Stop", "High" lost);
the rule was taken out. 188 agree; 4 kept, all older than this change.
