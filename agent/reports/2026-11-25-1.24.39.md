# 2026-11-25 — 1.24.39: PDFs as any viewer shows them

Branch `lantern-1.24.39` (from `lantern-1.24.38`, with its gradebook and
`train/` commits). Work branch `wip-2026-11-25-pdf`.

## What prompted it

Adam, 2026-11-25, on a work laptop kept off the internet at a maintenance
base (machine manuals, print drawings, PLC programs, filled-in forms):

- "Lantern should have all the capabilities that adobe acrobat pro has check
  and see if it does" -- the answer went to him as a PDF ("Lantern and
  Acrobat Pro"): of 42 things on Adobe's list, 4 done, 6 in part, 29 not, 3
  Adobe's cloud;
- then: "make lantern so that it can perform everything acrobat pro does as
  much as is possible".

The plan in the queue (`agent/QUEUE.md`, "Next track: PDF work to Acrobat
Pro's reach") has three steps. This version is the first: read every PDF as a
viewer does. 1.24.40 (the PDF tile and its tools) follows at once.

## What changed

See `CHANGELOG.md`. In short: the PDF password lock of every revision opens
(no question where only changes are locked; a password asked where one is
needed; certificate, unknown and damaged locks named and refused); filled-in
forms, comments, highlights, stamps and notes are drawn and read, with their
own drawings or Lantern's; a drawing's layers that are off are left out;
bookmarks and links are read; text strings in the PDF's own letters and in
UTF-8; every trailer of a file read (linearized PDFs). Text Reader asks for a
password and reads a scanned page with a form filled in on top.

## Reviews

- First (an agent that had not seen it written): 373 cases on 56 PDFs of its
  own; 43 failed, of 14 causes (`tests/fixtures/pdf_review1_1239/REPORT.txt`).
  The worst: a linearized locked PDF opened as blank pages with no error. All
  mended; its cases all pass, in Node and in Chromium.
- Second (another agent, on the mends): 376 checks on 115 PDFs of its own;
  26 failed, 9 of them regressions from 1.24.38 (Text Reader no longer read a
  scan with a note on it; an ordinary PDF refused as locked because its page
  shows the word "trailer"; Screen and Lighten wrong over the paper; a
  visibility expression naming itself froze the tab). All mended
  (`tests/fixtures/pdf_review2_1239/REPORT.txt`); every check agrees but one,
  kept with its reason (words at 60% as poppler draws them).
- Third (another agent, on the second round): 182 checks on 73 PDFs, from
  LibreOffice, cairo, pdfTeX and matplotlib as well as by hand; 20 failed, 3
  of them regressions: a LibreOffice page with 90 see-through shapes took 12 s
  to draw (28 ms before); a blend mode four forms deep wiped its drawing; a
  page leaving a clip and a q open came out see-through. Also every LaTeX
  PDF's letters were torn (in 1.24.38 too), and a PDF attached unpacked inside
  another was read as the document. All mended; every check agrees but three
  by design (`tests/fixtures/pdf_review3_1239/REPORT.txt`).
- Found by the review of 1.24.40's tools while these ran: a PDF with more than
  one object stream opened as no pages (in 1.24.38 too). Mended here.
