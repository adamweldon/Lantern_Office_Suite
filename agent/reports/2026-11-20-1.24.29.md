## What changed

- SHEET_EDIT: a press on the grid selects no text; drags over cells, row
  numbers and column letters; Shift+click on them; the corner; Shift+Space
  and Ctrl+Space; the selection's numbers and letters shaded; whole rows and
  columns not widened by merged cells; the grid takes the keys after a
  press; Shift+arrow over hidden rows; a drag while a formula is written
  puts in a range (SHEET: Point mode, a key typed starts the typing).
- SHEET_MENU (new): the right-click menus, Insert.../Delete... with Excel's
  choices, Format Cells (with Merge cells), Row Height, Column Width, Hide,
  Unhide, a long press on a phone, Ctrl+1, Ctrl++, Ctrl+-.
- SHEET_ROWS: Insert and Delete with a band (cells shifted); heights and
  hidden rows move with rows. SHEET_LAYOUT: heights, hidden rows and
  columns, widths in proportion to the grid's columns.
- SHEET, SHEET_FN_*: SUBTOTAL 101-111 and AGGREGATE leave hidden rows out.
  XLSX_READ/XLSX_WRITE: hidden rows and columns, rows' heights.
- PHYSICS_CELLS: LORE and LORENTZ; GAMMA is Excel's gamma function
  (SHEET_FN_MORE). CONVERT_EXCEL (new): Excel's CONVERT, one meaning for
  each unit name everywhere; XLSX_WRITE writes Excel's unit names.
- SHEET_FN_*: FIXED, DOLLAR, TEXTBEFORE/TEXTAFTER, FREQUENCY, ROMAN,
  ADDRESS, TIMEVALUE as Excel.
- AI_SHEET: [B2] read as the cell within the review's bounds; names in
  brackets, pipes, stacked summaries, text formulas, 26 columns.

- SHEET_EDIT, round 3: sheets named in other alphabets keep their names when
  rows go in; one undo list for the workbook (Excel's), each other sheet's
  cells taken back cell by cell; a row number or column letter pressed
  while pointing puts in 5:5 or C:C. SHEET: +$B$2 and +PI()*2 are
  formulas; SUBTOTAL and AGGREGATE pass over either anywhere in a formula.
  AI_SHEET: functions given named columns (SUMIF and the like take them
  whole), totals and subtotals, a column named in every row is the whole
  column, the copied-down check's lines. XLSX_WRITE: a unit given by a
  formula cell or joined texts read as Excel would, the note naming it.

Seven agents, none of which had seen the code written, made cases of their
own; each case was judged by what Excel does (Microsoft's documentation,
LibreOffice 24.2 and openpyxl standing in for Excel, which is not here).
Their cases are in `tests/fixtures/` and replayed by `npm test`.

The kept ones are of low or no harm: Point mode's Shift+click, Ctrl+click,
arrows and F4; Insert Copied and Cut Cells; Ctrl+9 (the browser's own key);
columns past Z; "pond" (not on Microsoft's list); ROMAN with TRUE or FALSE
(TRUE and FALSE are 1 and 0 here); GAMMA's 13th digit near 171; one width
Excel's own figure for could not be checked; one case that reads Summary
after Lantern, as Excel, has shown the sheet whose change Ctrl+Z took back.
