# 2026-11-06: the recipe with the question's numbers in it (unfinished, on `wip-2026-11-06-recipe-fill`)

**Unfinished, and not in any version.** This branch holds the filled
recipes that were written for 1.24.7 and taken out of it before it was
pushed. Two independent reviews each found them wrong for a large share of
questions put another way. They are kept here for the next attempt.

## What it is

A small model on the short prompt is shown a recipe (`=VOLTS({amps},{ohms})`)
and has to put the question's numbers into its blanks. On 1.24.5 and 1.24.6
the 2B and the 0.8B often could not: they declined, wrote the blank's name,
or took the wrong recipe's function. So Lantern was to fill the recipe
itself where it could tell which number goes in which blank
(`Lantern.aiNoFormula.filled`, the fillers and rules of `AI_RECIPE_FILL`)
and hand the model the finished line to copy.

## What the reviews found

A line handed over to copy has to be THE sum for the question. The fill
only knew that the numbers fitted the blanks.

- **First review** (199 questions, `tests/fixtures/fill_review_1247.json`):
  160 filled, 86 wrongly. The answer wanted in another unit ("in mA", "how
  many teaspoons of bleach" given the recipe for drops); another thing
  asked of the same numbers (the current a lamp draws, the other resistor,
  orbits a day, momentum); a step more (there and back, a week of days); a
  number in words or said twice; a radius read as a height.
- **Mended** with a rule for each card (what the question must ask for,
  what it must not say), a list of words that mean a step more, a strict
  test of the unit asked for, a count of the figures. On the first review's
  questions: 95 filled, all right.
- **Second review** (749 new questions, `tests/fixtures/fill_review2_1247.json`):
  about 400 filled, 222 of them not the sum for the question (250 by the
  stricter count in the file). The lists were not long enough and cannot
  be: "water from the dam", "from the creek", "milky water", "icy water",
  "for handwashing", "to soak instruments", "in my aquarium", "scented
  bleach" all got the drinking-water recipe for drops. The same in every
  card: a new word, a new wrong line.

## What the next attempt has to do

**Fill only a question whose every word Lantern knows.** A list of words to
refuse is never finished; a list of words to accept is. For each card: the
words of its plain question (its own nouns and verbs, the plain words of
asking), a number with its unit for each blank, and at most the one noun a
number describes ("a 60 W lamp", "a 12 V battery"). Any other word, and the
recipe goes to the model with its blanks, as on 1.24.6. `AI_SCENE` was
narrowed the same way after the same two reviews (its `plain()`), and the
second review's 127 right pictures then came through untouched.

Then, before it goes in: the two fixtures above replayed (no wrong line),
and a third reviewer who has not seen it.

Also still true on 1.24.6 and 1.24.7, and to be mended with this:
`recipeDiffers` there fills a recipe that has one blank where the question
has one number in the blank's unit, and tells a sum that differs to be the
recipe's. "How long should a dipole be for 14.2 MHz?" is told to write
`=WAVELEN(14.2)`. (From 1.24.7 it no longer does so for bleach: a card may
say `noFill`.)
