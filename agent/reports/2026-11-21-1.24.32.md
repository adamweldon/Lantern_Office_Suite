# 2026-11-21 — 1.24.32: a reply held to its block may stop when it is done

Started from `lantern-1.24.31`; worked on `wip-2026-11-21-grammar-end`. `main` is 1.24.25.

## Why

A scheduled check read the small models' run of 1.24.31 (run 44867258186) for Adam. The lift station passed on the
0.8B, the 2B and the 4B. But of the questions where Lantern held a reply to a block's grammar, the 0.8B passed 1 of
22, the 2B 4 of 17, the 4B 9 of 13, and 14 of the 38 failed held rows had no END and had run to the token limit
(the 2B's songs 95 and 174 bars, a checklist cut off, a drawing that never closed). The grammar ended in `"END"`, so
the end of the reply was not allowed anywhere else: a model that was done could not stop.

## What changed

- AI_ACTIONS `grammarFor`: `root ::= "MAKE K: " title "\n" (line "\n")* ("END" | line)?`. A free line is any line but
  END itself, so nothing follows END. Word-first lines are unchanged.
- tests: the run's failed rows saved (`tests/fixtures/bench_run_1.24.31_{0.8b,2b,4b}.json`); the 14 runaway replies,
  cut at their last whole line, are grammar samples now let stop (`tests/fixtures/grammar_1231/make_cases.js`);
  `tests/station.js` 53; the second and third station reviews keep GS-35 (no END) and F-26 (the free line's old form)
  with the reason, the second only for that one sample of its group.
- CHANGELOG: the two placeholder lines left at the end of 1.24.31's entry taken out.
