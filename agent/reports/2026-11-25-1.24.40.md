# 2026-11-25 — 1.24.40: the PDF tile

Branch `lantern-1.24.40` (from `lantern-1.24.39`). Work branch
`wip-2026-11-25-pdf`.

## What prompted it

Adam, 2026-11-25: "make lantern so that it can perform everything acrobat pro
does as much as is possible". 1.24.39 read every PDF as a viewer does; this
version is the PDF tile and the tools that change a PDF: the rest of the
plan's first two steps and most of its third (`agent/QUEUE.md`).

## What changed

See `CHANGELOG.md`. A PDF tile (find, zoom, panels, print, read aloud,
measure) and 15 tools, each making a new copy: Pages, Combine (PDFs and
pictures), Stamp (page numbers, Bates, header, footer, watermark), Fill in,
Comment, Sign, Flatten, Protect (AES-256), Redact, Remove hidden, Export
(pictures, words), Compare, Edit text, Reduce size, Make form. The PDF writer
under them writes only what the new file reaches, so nothing taken out stays
in the file. Standard fonts a PDF names without their widths are now read and
drawn by their own widths (words sit where other viewers put them).

Against Acrobat Pro's 42 (the table sent to Adam on 2026-11-25): before,
4 yes, 6 in part, 29 no; now 24 yes, 8 in part, 7 no (the same 3 Adobe's cloud).

  uses Adobe's own widths; a PDF naming another font without widths is still
  measured by the browser's nearest font.
