## What changed

- XLSX_READ (new): an .xlsx or .xlsm as sheets: values, formulas (shared,
  array, spilling; _xlfn. left out; names and tables' names as the cells
  they stand for; Excel's GAMMA as GAMMAFN; a plain formula's range where one
  value belongs with Excel 365's @), looks, widths, merges, the frozen row,
  the paper; what is not kept said in a note; Lantern's values set beside
  Excel's saved ones and the differences named.
- XLSX_WRITE (new): a sheet, or a workbook's sheets together, as an .xlsx:
  Excel's grammar checked for each formula (written in capitals, the rest of
  ROUND(A1) written out, POW and CEIL as POWER and CEILING; saved as text
  when Excel could not read it, as its value when Excel would work it out
  another way); array formulas and spilling ones (cm="1" and the metadata
  part); dates and fractions typed as Excel's numbers with their looks.
- SHEET_XLSX (new): Import file and Save .xlsx in Sheets; a workbook's sheets
  tied together (doc.book). GUIDE_SHEET_XLSX (new).
- FORMULA: in a sheet, Excel's order (minus, then per cent, then power,
  powers from the left); ranges and lists in { } worked out cell by cell,
  and a list given where a function takes one value worked out for each of
  its cells (SCALAR); errors kept in their cells; another sheet's cells
  (Jan!B6, 'Tax 2026'!B2, Jan:Mar!B2, Jan!B:B); @; NaN as #NUM!; a date with
  dots needs a year of four digits.
- SHEET: computeAll with the workbook's other sheets; sheetDocs.book and
  .kind (a cell's date or time) for the writer.
- SHEET_FN_*: BASE, DECIMAL, GAMMALN, GAMMAFN, GAMMA.DIST, ERROR.TYPE,
  UNIQUE, SORT, FILTER, SEQUENCE, TRANSPOSE; INDEX(range,0); INDIRECT to
  another sheet; SUMPRODUCT counts yes and no as 0s; a number format's
  currency in [$...] and its conditions.
- SHEET_LAYOUT (new): widths and merged cells on the grid. SHEET_EDIT: a
  range takes in a merged cell whole; copy, cut and paste carry merges.
  SHEET_ROWS: merges and widths move with rows and columns; a sort that
  would tear a merge is refused.
- AI_SHEET: Save .xlsx beside MAKE SHEET's Open in Sheets; two bench
  questions ask for an Excel file (make-sheet-excel, -b).
- AI_DOC: MAKE DOC's example begins with "## You need" (the 1.24.27 runs).
- tests/starter_live.py: looks four times a second while a download runs.
