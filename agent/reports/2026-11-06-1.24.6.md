## How the work went

- **Every failed row was replayed before anything was changed**, through a
  small harness (Lantern in jsdom, a model that says what the fixture says).
  For each kind of miss the question was: what did Lantern put in front of
  the model, and what did it send back? Most answers were in Lantern.
- **Where Lantern now sends something back, the reply that returns is
  written by hand.** So the tests show that the right thing goes back and
  that a right reply then passes. They do not show that a small model
  writes that reply. The run on GitHub does.
- **The 35B's 45 right sums of 1.24.4 are a guard**: every new check is run
  over them, and none may question one. It caught nothing this time; it is
  why the new checks could be added without a laptop run for each.
- **Nobody had looked at the minutes.** The short prompt of 1.24.5 made the
  2B's run three times as long, because the model server keeps only what
  two requests share from their first character. The instructions are now
  half the size; whether that is enough is in the next run's minutes.
- **What Lantern sends back is copied as surely as what it shows first.**
  Three of this version's fixes are to Lantern's own send-backs and
  examples (a note's sum, the tested example for "without delay", a button
  in a relay driver). The rule is now in `CLAUDE.md`.
- A full run on the laptop was started on the first finished build and
  stopped after 51 questions, when a picture showed a line of small print
  cut off; the puzzle and NEED work went in before the build was made
  again.
- **The 35B's one miss was the same question as on 1.24.5**, for another
  reason. Asked for a tune "8 bars long" it wrote eight bars of chords and
  four of melody, in the shape of the guide's four-bar example (as on

  **The full run was not repeated on the build that was pushed.**

## What a person, or a small model, can do now

- A person whose model answers "My brother is having a fit" with nonsense
  still sees what Lantern's lesson says, under the answer.
- "My hens have stopped laying eggs. What could be wrong?" is read against
  Lantern's lessons whatever the model does.

  Lantern, when the total is asked for.

## Found, and left

- **`=2000/8000` where `=2000/ANS` was meant** (the 4B, `water-days`, once):
  Lantern questioned it, said "ask with NEED" twice, and the 4B asked. One
  row once is not a finding; it is in the queue.
- **The number check is loose.** `=100*12/60` for "How many hours will my
  battery run a 60 W light?" is not questioned: 100 and 12 are among the
  factors a sum may use without the question giving them (percent, a
  dozen), and a number of the question times or over such a factor passes
  too (60/12 = 5). The bench catches it (it knows no battery was given);
  Lantern does not. Tightening it risks questioning right sums; it needs
  the guard and a run.
- **`LESSON:` for a program** was read the Volume lesson ("Tank fill/stop
  logic": "fill" in a title, "tank" in the text).
- **The guides' explanations are copied with their lines** by the 2B ("key C
