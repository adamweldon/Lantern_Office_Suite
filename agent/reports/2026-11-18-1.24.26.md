## What changed

- ZIP_INFLATE: DEFLATE unpacked in plain JavaScript (any browser; his 66-part
  .pptx in 17 ms), never past the size a part says.
- ZIP: parts read by the zip's list (names without regard to capitals),
  CRC-32 checked, zip64, UTF-16 XML parts, no part past 64 MB (a zip bomb);
  zips written stored. Both are for .xlsx and .pptx next.
- DOCX_PARTS, DOCX_TEXT, DOCX_READ: a .docx as a Writer document (see the
  CHANGELOG for all it reads) with a note of what Writer could not keep.
- DOCX_WRITE, DOCX_PACKAGE: a Writer document as a .docx, valid against the
  OOXML schema, on Writer's paper.
- WRITER_DOCX: Save .docx in Writer; Import file opens a .docx (WRITER_IMPORT
  turns away .doc, .odt, .rtf with how to make a .docx); the note above the
  page. GUIDE_WRITER_DOCX: the help.
- AI_DOC: Save .docx beside Save .pdf; MAKE DOC's instructions say it makes a
  Word file; "a Word document", "a .docx", "a docx" are documents.
- Bench: make-doc-word-checklist (set A) and make-doc-word-b (set B); the
  bench is 115 scored questions now. The check also reads back the Word file
  Lantern makes of the model's document.
