as written cannot be right and what it meant is plain. A number the model
typed stands, whatever its length. An arrow is read only before a recipe's
own label or the sum's own value, and only where every arrow of the reply
is one of these. A second = only for Lantern's own QUESTION line, with no
yes or no in it. A line that uses ANS of a line that could not run cannot
run either. The moon is not moved, nor the sun asked to be in front. A
recipe is held to only for a question in its own words and its unit.

`bench_1246_small.js` and `review3_1247.js` to `review7_1247.js`; on this
build none of them breaks (two that can be read either way are written
