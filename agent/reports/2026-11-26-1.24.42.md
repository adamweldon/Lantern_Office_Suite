# 1.24.42 -- the Photo tile (2026-11-26)

## What Adam asked
2026-11-26: "say so and do what the person wants. Lantern should also offer the same for
photoshop." The first half was 1.24.41 (a PDF's maker's limits). This is the second: a
picture editor that does on the device, with no internet, what Photoshop does, as far as
arithmetic can.

## What changed
- A new tile, Photo, of 53 modules (PHOTO_*, GUIDE_PHOTO), each droppable. Layers, groups,
  masks, 27 blending modes, History with the opening state kept; PSD/PSB both ways (layers,
  groups, masks, modes, the plain adjustment layers; 16- and 32-bit files with their
  layers); TIFF both ways; PNG, JPEG, WebP, GIF (animated), BMP, SVG, PDF with the
  resolution kept; 34 tools; 23 adjustments; 39 filters, Liquify, Lens Correction;
  Content-Aware Fill (PatchMatch), healing, Content-Aware Scale (seam carving), Merge
  Exposures (exposure fusion), Stack Mode; ten layer styles; eight custom shapes; a Swatches panel; the Pen and its paths;
  Actions and Batch; the help (GUIDE_PHOTO).
- CHANGELOG has the whole list and every mend.
