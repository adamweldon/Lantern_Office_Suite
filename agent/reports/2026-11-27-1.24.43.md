# 2026-11-27 — 1.24.43: the Video tile

Adam asked: "Lantern should have a video editor like adobe does".

## What changed

A new tile, Video (44 modules, VIDEO_* and GUIDE_VIDEO): a Premiere Pro-like editor that
opens MP4, MOV, WebM, MKV and AVI from the stick, edits them on a Timeline with linked
picture and sound, colours them (Lumetri), puts titles, captions, transitions and effects
on them, steadies a shaky shot, follows a face with a mask, slows a moment down (Time
Remapping, Maintain Audio Pitch), mixes their sound to a measured loudness, and writes MP4,
WebM, MOV, AVI, GIF, WAV, PNG frames, EDL and Final Cut Pro XML, with nothing sent anywhere.
See CHANGELOG.md 1.24.43 for the whole list and what the reviews found.

Against 65 things from Adobe's Premiere Pro user guide: 34 done, 15 in part, 8 not, 8
needing Adobe's servers or trained models (the PDF "Lantern and Premiere Pro").

## The reviews

Four agents that had not seen the tile written, in two rounds:
- round 1: review 1 (files and arithmetic, 1,061 checks) found 69 failures, review 2 (as a
  person uses it, 549 checks in Chromium) 54;
- round 2, on the mended build: review 3 (the newest parts' arithmetic, a fuzz of about
  13,000 edits, EDL and XML read by OpenTimelineIO, 269 checks) found 38, review 4 (as a
  person uses it again, 270 checks) 27.

All mended but 15, each kept failing with its reason in `tests/review_video_1243.py`
(5 from round 1, 10 from round 2: three fuzzes that copy the Timeline's old way of
trimming linked partners, two where the harness leaves out a WAV's channel mask, three
judgements, white noise 1.1 dB quieter at 133% with Maintain Audio Pitch, a colour 8 levels
off as this browser decodes it). The fixtures stay in `tests/fixtures/video_review*_1243`.
