## Why

## What changed

- PPTX_TEXT (new): a slide's text both ways -- PowerPoint's paragraphs (bullets and numbers from the paragraph, its
  list style, the layout's and master's placeholder, the master's text styles), runs and tables as HTML; a slide's
  HTML as DrawingML paragraphs and tables.
- PPTX_READ (new): `Lantern.pptx.read(bytes, name)` -- titles (a title box, or a short first line said), text in
  reading order, tables, charts as tables of their numbers, SmartArt as lists of its words, the largest picture a
  browser shows, notes; footers left; what could not come in said with the slides.
- PPTX_WRITE (new): `Lantern.pptx.write(deck)` / `save(deck)` -- 16:9, title and content placeholders, lists,
  looks, links, tables, the picture beside the text, notes slides, the deck's colours as the theme.
- SLIDES_PPTX and GUIDE_SLIDES_PPTX (new): Save .pptx in a deck, Open .pptx in the list (pictures made a JPEG of at
  most 1,200 pixels, as Slides makes one), a dialog of what was not kept; the Slides guide's section.
- EMF_DRAW (new): `Lantern.emf.draw(bytes, max)` -- an EMF picture played on a canvas (Visio drawings), as PNG.
- AI_SLIDES: Save .pptx in MAKE SLIDES' result; `also` in the command list.
- AI_ACTIONS and AI: a reply the model ended itself ("stop") closes its last block, unless its last paragraph is one
  sentence of prose; an open program or table leaves such a sentence outside it (1.24.32's run: a whole oil change
  was asked to be shorter). The failed rows of 1.24.32's run saved as fixtures.
- CLAUDE.md: wait for the version branch's run to be listed before pushing its wip branch (1.24.32's first push
  started nothing).
