The high findings ran 47, 37, 38, 26, 41 and 51. The count did not fall: each
reviewer wrote new wordings, not the earlier ones again. Every guess at
fitting a given (a light's state, a station count, a sequence's order, a time
in words) was copied wrong by somebody's phrasing. The mend that held was
refusing, not fitting more:

- a given now needs every word of the question to be one it knows;
- it needs one machine;
- it needs what it fits to be read surely.

The code checks had the same lesson. They now ask what the switch stops: a
machine, never an alarm, a fan, a valve or a timer. They judge the contact as
it is drawn.

All replays agree but the kept cases, each with its reason in its wrapper. The
older reviews (1.24.30 to 1.24.35) keep what changed by design.
