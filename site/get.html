Get Lantern

An office and learning suite with its own AI. Free, and it works without the internet once it is on your computer or phone.

Lantern for Windows

Download Lantern (7 MB)
Your download has started. If it did not, use the button.
  1. Open the file you downloaded (Get-Lantern.exe), from the bar at the bottom or top of your browser, or from Downloads. If the browser asks whether to keep it, choose Keep.
  2. If Windows says “Windows protected your PC”, click More info, then Run anyway.
    Windows says this about any program that has not paid for a signing certificate yet. Lantern is free, and all of its code is public on GitHub.
  3. Lantern opens in your browser straight away, and you can use it. Its AI downloads in the background (about 3 GB). The button in the bottom corner of Lantern shows how far it has got. Leave the computer on and connected until it says everything is here.

Next time, double-click Lantern on your desktop or in the Start menu. Lantern is kept in a folder called Lantern in your user folder.

Lantern for a Mac

Download Lantern (6 MB)
Your download has started. If it did not, use the button.
  1. Open Downloads and double-click Lantern. (If you see Get-Lantern-Mac.zip instead, double-click that first, and it becomes Lantern.)
  2. The first time, the Mac says it cannot check Lantern. Click Done. Then open System Settings, choose Privacy & Security, scroll down, click Open Anyway next to Lantern, and enter your password.
    The Mac says this about any program that has not paid Apple to be checked yet. Lantern is free, and all of its code is public on GitHub. You only do this once.
  3. Lantern opens in your browser straight away, and you can use it. Its AI downloads in the background (about 3 GB). The button in the bottom corner of Lantern shows how far it has got.

To keep it handy, drag Lantern into Applications. Next time, open Lantern from there. Lantern is kept in a folder called Lantern in your home folder. It needs macOS 11 or newer.

Lantern for Linux

Download Lantern (7 MB)
For a Raspberry Pi or another ARM computer: the ARM download.
Your download has started. If it did not, use the button.
  1. A downloaded file is not allowed to run until you say so. In a terminal, in your Downloads folder:
    chmod +x Get-Lantern-Linux-x64 && ./Get-Lantern-Linux-x64
    Or in the file manager: right-click it, Properties, Permissions, tick “Allow executing as a program”, then double-click it.
  2. Lantern opens in your browser straight away. Its AI downloads in the background; the button in the bottom corner shows how far it has got.

Next time, open Lantern from your applications menu. Lantern is kept in ~/Lantern.

Lantern on an Android phone or tablet

Open Lantern
  1. In Chrome, open Lantern with the button above.
  2. Tap the menu (⋮), then Install app or Add to Home screen.
  3. Lantern now works without the internet. For its AI, open the AI app inside Lantern and download a model once (the small one is about 0.5 GB; use Wi-Fi).

Lantern on an iPhone or iPad

Open Lantern
  1. Open Lantern in Safari with the button above.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Lantern then works without the internet. For its AI, open the AI app inside Lantern and download a model once (the small one is about 0.5 GB; use Wi-Fi).

Lantern on a Chromebook

Open Lantern
  1. Open Lantern with the button above.
  2. Click the install icon at the right of the address bar, or the menu (⋮), then Install Lantern.
  3. For its AI, open the AI app inside Lantern and download a model once.

What it needs

The internet for the first download only. About 4 GB of free space for Lantern and its AI. The AI that is downloaded is the one your computer’s memory suits: a small one first, so Chat works within minutes, then a stronger one.

Once its AI is in, Lantern can also add the encyclopedia (Wikipedia, 12 GB) and books on medicine, repair, travel and science (5 GB). It asks first, and says how big each is.

Lantern is free and its code is public: github.com/adamweldon/Lantern_Office_Suite. Nothing you write in it leaves your computer.
